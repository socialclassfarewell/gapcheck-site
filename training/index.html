Gap Check training: two ways to learn

The same NDIS courses and the same training record, in two formats. Pick the one that suits your team.

Gap Check Simple

Clean, quick training with nothing extra.

  • Short lessons you read on a phone or computer
  • A "check yourself" question inside lessons, with feedback straight away
  • A course quiz at the end
  • A record of completion in your worker register

For teams that want training done with no fuss.

Gap Check Interactive

The same courses, with more to do in each lesson.

  • Progress beads show where you are in every lesson
  • Most lessons include a hands-on practice step: fill the gap, match up, or pick all that apply
  • Practice is never marked, so workers can try again as often as they like
  • "Up next" shows what to do after each lesson, and where you left off
  • The same course quiz and the same record of completion

For teams that want lessons workers do, not just read.
